Your result - Solid foundation

You have a consistent, structured approach to exit interviews and the data is being reviewed. The next step is closing the loop - linking what employees tell you to specific retention actions. Use this template to probe deeper on the themes you're already seeing, and look for the patterns behind the patterns.

1. Role and Day-to-Day Experience
What did you enjoy most about your role here?
What aspects of the role were most frustrating or draining?
Did the role match what was described when you were hired?
2. Team and Management
How would you describe your relationship with your direct manager?
Did you feel supported and recognised in your work?
Was there anything about the team dynamic that affected your experience?
3. Growth and Development
Did you have opportunities to learn and grow in this role?
Was there a clear path forward for your career here?
What would have made you more likely to stay?
4. Culture and Environment
How would you describe the overall company culture?
Did you feel comfortable raising concerns or sharing ideas?
Was there anything about the environment that surprised you - positively or negatively?
5. The Real Reason
What is the primary reason you decided to leave?
Give the employee time to answer fully - this is the most valuable part of the conversation.
Was there a specific moment or event that prompted your decision?
Listen without interrupting - often the first answer is not the full answer.
If you could change one thing about how things are done here, what would it be?
This is an open door - take notes carefully.
6. Looking Ahead
Would you recommend this company as a place to work? Why or why not?
Is there anything you'd like leadership to know that wasn't covered here?
Would you be open to returning in the future under different circumstances?
