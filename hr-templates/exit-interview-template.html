    Things people ask

    Most employees soften exit feedback to protect professional relationships and references. The conditions for honesty - a neutral interviewer, genuine anonymity, and visible follow-through - are rarely all present at the same time.
    HR, not the employee's direct manager. A neutral party consistently produces more candid responses. For senior exits, some organisations use an external facilitator to add an additional layer of independence.
    Collect feedback in a standardised format across all exits so responses are comparable over time. Look for recurring themes, not individual responses. Share findings with leadership quarterly - the visibility of follow-through is what builds trust in the process.
